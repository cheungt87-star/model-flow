Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Modelflow (“Modelflow”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you visit modelflow.ae (the “Website”). We keep it deliberately simple, because the Website collects very little.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, sign-ups or sign-ins.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • The only way to give us personal data is to send us an enquiry by email.

Who we are

Modelflow, Meydan Grandstand, 6th floor, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, U.A.E. Email: info@modelflow.ae. We are the controller of the personal data described below.

Enquiries by email

The contact form on the Website asks for your name, email address and message. When you press “Send message”, the form opens a ready-to-send email in your own email application, addressed to info@modelflow.ae. Nothing is sent to us, or stored by the Website, until you send that email yourself. You can also email us directly at any time.

When we receive your email, we process your name, email address, the content of your message and anything else you choose to include, only to read and respond to your enquiry and to keep a reasonable record of it. Please do not include sensitive information (such as identity documents, financial account details or health data) in an enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

The Website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising technologies, and does not build profiles of visitors. If this ever changes, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Technical data and service providers

To work at all, any website must handle some technical data. On the Website this happens in two ways:

  • Hosting. Our hosting provider serves the Website and may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of request) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.
  • Fonts. The Website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of that request.

Our email service provider also processes the enquiries you send us.

How we use personal data

  • To respond to your enquiry and follow up where needed.
  • To keep the Website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations and to protect our legal rights.

We rely on your consent when you send us an enquiry, and on our legitimate interest in running a secure website and responding to business contacts.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for marketing. We share it only with service providers who host the Website or handle our email, under appropriate confidentiality and security terms, and where required by law or a competent authority. Some providers may process data outside the United Arab Emirates; where that happens we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected.

Retention

We keep enquiries only for as long as needed to deal with them and for reasonable record-keeping, and then delete them or anonymise them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your rights

Under the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and other applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise a right, email info@modelflow.ae. We may need to confirm your identity first. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Website is intended for business visitors and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Links to other sites

If the Website links to other sites, we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the current version.

Contact
