About us

Advisory and trade, built for global business.

Modelflow is a Dubai-based company licensed by Meydan. We engage in international commercial activity and provide business-to-business operational advisory services, helping enterprises grow and streamline how they work across borders.

Management consultancyOperational process optimisation, strategic planning and international expansion, IT and digital workflow advisory, and market research with vendor management.
General tradingWholesale trade of non-regulated commercial goods, including IT equipment and office electronics, commercial software and standard consumer and commercial goods. We do not deal in regulated, controlled or restricted commodities.
